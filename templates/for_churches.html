Common questions

Before you ask.

Is the monthly plan required?

Yes. The build is half of it. Hosting, security, updates, and dashboard access live in the partnership, and churches that skip ongoing support end up with the same stale site they started with.

Do we have to replace our church management system?

No. We integrate with what you already pay for. Replacing it is a decision we would only recommend after the assessment, and only if the math favors it.

What if we already have a website?

We audit it first. If the foundation is sound we work with it. If it costs more to maintain than to replace, we say so.

How long does a build take?

Most launches run four to six weeks from kickoff, depending on how quickly we get content, photos, and feedback from your team.

Can we change plans later?

Move up or down at any time. Most churches start on Foundation and move to Connect once the app is the next real need.
