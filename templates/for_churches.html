What we build

Six functions, one system.

01 Planning

Service planning and implementation

We map how your Sunday gets built, then remove the steps that do not need a human. Volunteer scheduling, run-of-show, rehearsal timelines, and a documented workflow your team can run without you in the room.

02 Build

Website and app development

A mobile-first church website and a matching app. Sermon library, events, giving, push notifications, and a resource hub that gives members a reason to open it on a Tuesday.

03 Automate

Communication automation

Sequences for first-time guests, new members, group signups, and giving follow-up. Written once, sent on time, every time, without adding a task to anyone's list.

04 Discipleship

Discipleship pathways

A defined path from first visit to disciple-maker, the resources to teach each stage, and the tracking to see who is moving. Growth becomes a number your leaders can act on.

05 Finance

Financial audits and projections

A review of where the money goes, a budget your board can defend, and a forecast showing what next year's hire or building project actually requires.

06 Grants

Google for Nonprofits setup

We run the application, configure Workspace, enable the nonprofit YouTube tier, and enroll you in Google Ad Grants. Approved organizations receive up to $10,000 per month in search advertising.
