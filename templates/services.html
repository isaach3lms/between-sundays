Services

Operational support, one function at a time.

Six areas of work that churches typically hire six different people to cover. We handle them as one engagement, with one point of contact.

01 Planning

Service planning and implementation

We map how your Sunday gets built, then remove the steps that do not need a human. Volunteer scheduling, run-of-show, rehearsal timelines, and a documented workflow your team can run without you in the room.

02 Build

Website and app development

A modern, mobile-first church website and a matching mobile app. Sermon library, events, giving, push notifications, and a resource hub that keeps your congregation connected during the week, not just on the weekend.

03 Automate

Communication automation

Automated email and text sequences for first-time guests, new members, small group signups, and giving follow-up. Written once, sent on time, every time, without adding a task to anyone's list.

04 Discipleship

Discipleship systems

Custom pathways from first visit to disciple-maker, with the resources to teach each stage and the tracking to see who is moving. Your leaders get a clear picture of spiritual growth instead of a hunch.

05 Finance

Financial audits and projections

A review of where the money goes, a budget your board can defend, and a forecast that shows what next year's hire or building project actually requires. Stewardship strategy grounded in real numbers.

06 Grants

Google for Nonprofits setup

We run the application, configure Google Workspace, enable the nonprofit YouTube tier, and enroll your church in Google Ad Grants. Approved churches receive up to $10,000 per month in search advertising at no cost.

The dashboard

One view of the whole congregation.

Every partnership includes the Between Sundays Church Dashboard. Manage your website and app, monitor discipleship pathways, organize ministry resources, and track the people your systems usually lose.

  • Who is a first-time guest?
  • Who has completed our membership class?
  • Who is serving?
  • Who is in a small group?
  • Who has been baptized?
  • Who is being discipled?
  • Who is discipling others?
