About

Pastors were called to shepherd people, not troubleshoot software.

Between Sundays exists to take the administrative, technical, and operational work off the desks of church leaders, and to do it well enough that they stop thinking about it.

Mission

Equip churches to operate all week.

To give churches the tools, systems, and expertise they need to run effectively throughout the week, so pastors can focus on leading people closer to Christ.

Vision

Become the trusted operational partner.

Healthier ministries, more efficient operations, and churches better equipped to reach the communities they were planted in.

Why it matters

An extension of your staff, not another vendor.

Hiring a web developer, a communications director, a data analyst, and a bookkeeper is out of reach for most churches. Between Sundays delivers that professional support as one partnership, at a fraction of the cost of one full-time hire.

How we work

Four commitments.

Ministry first

Every recommendation is measured against one question: does this give leaders more time with people? If it does not, we do not build it.

Plain answers

No jargon, no upsell. If a church does not need something, we say so on the first call, before there is a proposal in front of anyone.

Systems you own

We build so your team can run it. Training is included, documentation is written in your language, and nothing is hostage to us.

Long-term partnership

The build is the start. We stay on to maintain, measure, and adjust as the church grows.

Long-term goal

Healthy ministry every day of the week.

To become the premier church operations partner, equipping churches across the country with the systems, technology, and strategy that make sustainable ministry possible, not just on Sundays.
