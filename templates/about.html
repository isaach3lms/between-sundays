About

A decade in the church, not in a software company.

Between Sundays exists to give small churches the discipleship systems that large ones pay staff to run.

Hey there, I am Isaac, founder of Between Sundays.

For the last decade I have served the local and global church through teaching, building discipleship systems and strategies, and creative arts. I have had the privilege of working with churches and ministries like Life.Church, Seacoast Church, YouVersion, and Rock City Church, and I have helped plant several smaller churches along the way.

My heart is to serve small local churches that do not have the budget to hire full-time staff. When we work together, you are not just buying software. You are also adding a pastor to your team.

Together we will build a discipleship system that shows where every person is on their journey and what their next step is, without paying for multiple systems that never show the full picture of your church.
