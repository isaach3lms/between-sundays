The problem

Your impact is real. Your reporting cannot prove it.

Donor records in one platform, volunteer signups in a form, program participants in a spreadsheet that one person maintains. When a funder asks how many people you served and what happened to them, the answer takes a week to assemble and still comes with a caveat.

Donor attrition nobody sees

A first-time donor who never gets a second ask is indistinguishable from one who declined. Both are just gone.

Volunteers who fade out

People sign up, serve twice, and disappear. Without a record, no one notices until the schedule breaks.

Outcomes you cannot report

Grant applications ask for longitudinal outcomes. Spreadsheets give you a headcount.

What we build

Six functions, one system.

01 Donors

Donor systems and stewardship

Giving history, recurring donor tracking, lapsed-donor alerts, and automated acknowledgment. Every gift is receipted and every donor has a defined next touch.

02 Volunteers

Volunteer coordination

Signup, scheduling, reminders, and hour tracking in one place. Hours roll straight into board and grant reporting instead of being reconstructed at year end.

03 Programs

Participant tracking and outcomes

Intake through completion, with the milestones that matter to your mission recorded along the way. Longitudinal outcomes become a report, not a research project.

Financial audits and projections

Budget review, revenue forecasting, and program cost analysis. Know your cost per person served before the next grant cycle, not after.
