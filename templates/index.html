The problem

Most pastors are doing two jobs.

One is ministry. The other is running a small technology company: the website nobody updates, the app that never launched, the giving platform that does not talk to the database, the guest who visited three weeks ago and was never contacted. That second job has no budget for a full-time hire, so it lands on the person who was called to preach.

Hours, not minutes

Weekly service prep, announcements, graphics, and follow-up consume staff time that was budgeted for people.

Systems that do not connect

Attendance in one place, giving in another, small groups on a spreadsheet. No single view of the congregation.

Growth without structure

New people arrive faster than the follow-up process can absorb them, and quietly leave the same way.
